Proposed Chapter Structure & Concept Inventory
Chapter 1: Optimal Stopping (001.json)
Target: 8 Questions

Core Concepts: The 37% Rule, Look-Then-Leap Rule, Odds Algorithm.

Sub-topics & Case Studies: The Secretary Problem, renting an apartment in San Francisco, dating/marriage thresholds, selling a house.

Scenario Approach: Personal and everyday dilemmas where you need to maximize your chances of picking the absolute best option without looking backward.

Chapter 2: Explore vs. Exploit (002.json)
Target: 9 Questions

Core Concepts: Regret Minimization, Win-Stay/Lose-Shift, Gittins Index, Upper Confidence Bound.

Sub-topics & Case Studies: Multi-Armed Bandit problem, A/B testing, clinical drug trials, deciding where to eat dinner based on your remaining lifespan.

Scenario Approach: Striking the balance between trying new things (high uncertainty, high potential) versus enjoying known favorites (low risk, predictable return).

Chapter 3: Sorting & Searching (003.json)
Target: 10 Questions

Core Concepts: Big-O Notation, Comparison Sorts (Bubble, Insertion, Merge Sort), Search Costs.

Sub-topics & Case Studies: The physical layout of libraries, sorting a messy laundry pile, indexing files, the evolutionary cost of social hierarchies (sorting people).

Scenario Approach: Designing systems to organize your physical or digital life, balancing the time it takes to sort something vs. the time it takes to find it later.

Chapter 4: Caching & Memory Management (004.json)
Target: 8 Questions

Core Concepts: Memory Hierarchy, Eviction Policies, Least Recently Used (LRU), Least Frequently Used (LFU), Random Eviction.

Sub-topics & Case Studies: Belady's Anomaly, the physical desktop workspace, the closet organization trick (hanger flipping), human forgetting curves as an optimal filter.

Scenario Approach: Productivity and spatial optimization—how to manage limited space (closets, desks, mental bandwidth) using data-driven rules.

Chapter 5: Scheduling & Time Management (005.json)
Target: 10 Questions

Core Concepts: Earliest Due Date (EDD), Shortest Processing Time (SPT), Moore's Algorithm, Context Switching, Thread Thrashing.

Sub-topics & Case Studies: Interrupt coalescing, minimizing maximum lateness vs. minimizing the total number of late tasks, the cost of multi-tasking.

Scenario Approach: Professional and creative workflows. Handling a massive inbox, juggling multiple freelance clients, or managing a chaotic household to-do list.

Chapter 6: Overfitting & Relaxation (006.json)
Target: 9 Questions

Core Concepts: Overfitting, Regularization (Lasso/Ridge), Occam's Razor, Constraint Relaxation, Continuous Relaxation.

Sub-topics & Case Studies: The "Less-is-More" effect in sports/business, data modeling errors, traveling salesman problem approximations, making decisions when a perfect solution is mathematically impossible.

Scenario Approach: Strategic planning and decision-making under intense pressure. Knowing when to look at fewer metrics to make a better choice.

Chapter 7: Game Theory & Mechanism Design (007.json)
Target: 10 Questions

Core Concepts: Nash Equilibrium, Dominant Strategy, Prisoner's Dilemma, Mechanism Design, Information Asymmetry, Vickrey Auctions.

Sub-topics & Case Studies: Tragedy of the Commons, evolutionary stable strategies, designing a kid's chore system where they don't cheat, auction sniping.

Scenario Approach: Social dynamics and ethical choices. Designing "rules of the game" in families, small businesses, or community groups so that selfish behavior accidentally leads to a cooperative outcome.

Scenario Diversity Matrix
To ensure the quiz feels real and avoids getting bogged down in corporate/office examples (adhering to Step 7):

~30% Professional/Technical: Code debugging, project management, managing client queues.

~40% Everyday/Personal: Finding parking spots, cleaning out the garage, dating, choosing a restaurant.

~30% Creative, Social & Philosophical: Art projects, resolving family arguments, community resource sharing.
